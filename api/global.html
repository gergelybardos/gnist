 50 Strength of the radial attraction/repulsion. Positive values pull inward, negative values push outward.
damping number <optional>
 0.05 Velocity damping factor applied near the vortex center to stabilize motion and prevent particle overshooting.
radius number <optional>
 Infinity Radius of influence. Particles outside this distance are unaffected.
cullingRadius number <optional>
 0 Distance threshold from the vortex center below which particles are marked dead, surrounded by a frame-rate independent soft-aging buffer zone to prevent visual popping. Note that high particle `speed` or long particle `lifespan`, and/or vortexes with small or zero `damping` may cause particles to mathematically bypass the vortex center and slingshot outward. One way of preventing this slingshot effect is to tweak mentioned parameters. The other is to increase `cullingRadius` to intercept particles before they reach their escape brink. Omit or set to 0 to disable. Stored internally as Vortex#cullingRadius and as a squared value in Vortex#cullingRadiusSquared.
